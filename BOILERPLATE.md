# Describing Gaze

Use these as written. If you need to say something that is not here, ask us first: admin@graviton.xyz.

## One line

> Gaze: your attention, rewarded.

## Short

> Gaze is a watch protocol on Solana. It follows attention on the platforms you already use.

## Longer

> Gaze is a watch protocol on Solana. It follows attention on the platforms you already use, and works in your browser
> with no phone app. Gaze is built by Graviton Inc.
