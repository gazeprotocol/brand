# Using the Gaze mark

The eye travels with the name until people know it on its own.

## The four pieces

| Piece | Files | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Eye + wordmark** (primary) | `logo/svg/gaze-lockup-eye-{dark,light,afterimage}.svg`, plus `-small` versions under 20px cap height | The default: nav bars, partner pages, slides, email and document headers |
| **Eye over wordmark** (hero) | `logo/svg/gaze-lockup-stacked-{dark,light}.svg` | Once per piece, big: covers, posters, video end cards, event backdrops |
| **The eye alone** (icon) | `logo/svg/gaze-mark*.svg` | Only as an icon or avatar, or as art when the wordmark is already in view |
| **Dot + wordmark** (quiet) | `logo/svg/gaze-lockup-dot-{dark,light}.svg` | Footers, running heads, dense layouts |

Need "PROTOCOL · SOLANA" under the name? Use `gaze-lockup-horizontal-{dark,light}.svg`.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space equal to the eye's height around the mark, and the wordmark's cap height around lockups.
- Minimum sizes: the eye 16px, eye + wordmark 120px wide, stacked 200px wide, dot + wordmark 96px wide.

## Do

- Put the full-color eye on obsidian or another dark background, with room to breathe.
- Use the mono ink eye on warm-white and other light backgrounds.
- Use the files as they are.

## Don't

- Rotate, skew, stretch, flip or animate the eye's shape.
- Put the full-color, glowing eye on a light background.
- Place the mark on busy photos without a dark overlay of at least 72%.
- Put the eye inside a circle, badge or other container (full-bleed app icons excepted).
- Recolor the gradient, gradient-fill the wordmark, or add outlines, shadows or bevels.
- Use the eye as a repeating pattern, or show two big eyes in one view.
- Pair the mark with hype, price talk or exclamation marks.

## Co-branding

When Gaze appears next to another logo, use the eye + wordmark lockup at a similar visual size to the partner mark,
separated by clear space or a thin divider. Do not combine the marks into one. Ask us first: admin@graviton.xyz.
